Exam Day Strategies
  • TRUNCATE vs DELETE: TRUNCATE is DDL, resets AUTO_INCREMENT, and cannot be rolled back in MySQL.
  • WHERE vs HAVING: WHERE filters individual rows before grouping; HAVING filters groups after.
  • NULL Checks: Never use = NULL. Always use IS NULL.
  • Window Functions: RANK() leaves gaps on ties (1,1,3); DENSE_RANK() does not (1,1,2).
